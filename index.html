Story Bible & Character Dossiers

L

Lyra Thorne

Protagonist • Astral Weaver

Mortal scholar and secret Astral Weaver carrying the lineage of celestial light. Independent, stubborn, and courageous.

  • Magic: Star-weaving, radiant shields, astral blades
  • Tether Effect: Radiates endless cosmic warmth
K

Lord Kaelen Vane

Co-Protagonist • Shadow Warden

High Warden of Nocturne Reach. A 300-year-old Fae Commander cursed with carrying the devouring Void inside his soul.

  • Magic: Umbral siphoning, shadow-stepping, void flame
  • Tether Effect: Quietens the hungering Void in his heart

Cosmology of Vaelen

Vaelen is split between the mortal realm of Solara and the nocturnal realm of Umbra. The Great Rupture tore open the Void Well, threatening both realms with anti-existence.

Astral Magic

Harvested from stars. Generates heat and radiant energy. Drains wielder's body temperature.

Umbral Magic

Harvested from the Void. Absorbs warmth and emotion, turning wielder's heart into ice.
