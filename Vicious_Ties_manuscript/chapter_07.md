# Chapter 7: Secrets in the Shadows

By late afternoon, Blackwood Manor was bustling with controlled, tactical activity.

Marcus Kane and twelve elite Vance Syndicate operators were prepping weapons in the subterranean armory beneath the West Wing. Heavy cases of tactical gear, satellite radios, Kevlar body armor, night-vision goggles, and high-caliber suppressed rifles lined the cold stone walls. The atmosphere was charged with military precision.

In the private shooting range tucked beneath the East Wing, Killian stood close behind Nora, his broad chest pressing against her back, his long arms wrapped around hers as he guided her grip on a heavy tactical shotgun.

"Feet shoulder-width apart, Little Bird," Killian instructed softly, his chest vibrating against her spine, his warm breath fanning her ear. "Lean your weight forward into the recoil. Don't let the weapon control you; you control the weapon with every breath."

Nora adjusted her stance, taking a deep, steady breath, aligning the front sight post with the red silhouette downrange. She squeezed the trigger.

*BOOM.*

The heavy steel target thirty yards downrange buckled under the blast, hit dead-center.

"Impressive," Killian whispered against her neck, slipping his left hand up her ribcage, pressing a warm, lingering kiss to the side of her jaw. "You shoot like someone who was born to fight."

Nora smiled, resting her head back against his broad, muscular shoulder. "I had a strict teacher."

The heavy steel door of the firing range clicked open. Marcus Kane walked in, holding an encrypted military tablet displaying a flashing satellite map.

"Boss," Marcus interrupted grimly, his tone businesslike. "Our intelligence team tracked Moretti’s satellite phone ping. He's holed up in a secure warehouse facility at Pier 14 on the Portland docks, preparing to flee the country on a Liberian freighter in less than two hours."

Killian’s eyes narrowed into slits of lethal steel. The affectionate lover vanished instantly, replaced by the dangerous, calculating sovereign of Vance Enterprises. He stepped away from Nora, taking the tablet from Marcus.

"Gather the primary strike team," Killian ordered, his voice cold and commanding. "Three vehicles. Heavy tactical armor. We leave in ten minutes. No survivors."

"Wait!" Nora spoke up, stepping forward, setting the tactical shotgun on the bench. "I'm coming with you."

Killian turned to her, his jaw tightening instantly. "Absolutely not. You are staying at Blackwood Manor under heavy guard."

"Killian, Victor Moretti ordered the murder of my father!" Nora demanded, her amber eyes blazing with fierce, unyielding resolve. "I'm not sitting in a luxury suite drinking wine while you fight the man who destroyed my family. I rode in the plane with you to Maine, and I'm riding in the car to Portland. I have a stake in this reckoning, and I will not be locked away."

Killian stared at her, his pulse pounding in his throat. He hated the thought of putting her anywhere near live gunfire, but he saw the unyielding steel in her eyes—the same fiery courage that had captivated him six months ago when he first saw her photograph in Arthur Mercer's office.

He walked over to her slowly, grabbed a light Kevlar vest from the gear table, and carefully strapped it securely over her chest, cinching the side velcro straps tight until it fit like armor.

"You stay inside the armored Suburban with Marcus," Killian commanded softly, his hand lingering over her heart, feeling its steady rhythm. "If anything goes wrong, Marcus takes you straight to the private airfield. Understood?"

Nora touched his face gently, feeling the rough shadow stubble along his jawline. "Understood. Just promise me you come back to me alive."

Killian kissed her hard on the lips—a fierce, possessive promise against her mouth that left her tingling with desire. "I will always come back to you, Nora. You hold my soul."

Ten minutes later, they loaded into a convoy of three dark, armored Suburban SUVs with blacked-out glass. 

As the convoy sped down the foggy coastal highway toward Portland, Nora sat beside Killian in the darkened backseat, holding his gloved hand tightly. Killian checked his weapons with calm, lethal efficiency, racking slides and loading custom magazines, but his silver-blue eyes never stayed off Nora for long. He checked the straps of her Kevlar vest three times, ensuring she was completely protected from any stray shrapnel.

"You don't have to kill him, Killian," Nora murmured softly as the foggy skyline of Portland appeared through the ocean mist. "If we take him down legally through the FBI using the decrypted flash drive, Moretti goes to federal prison forever without shedding more blood."

"Moretti tried to take you from me, Nora," Killian said softly, his silver eyes flashing with dark, unyielding passion. "I will show him what happens to men who touch my queen."
