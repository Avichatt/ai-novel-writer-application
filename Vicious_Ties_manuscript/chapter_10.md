# Chapter 10: Vicious Devotion

Six months later.

The top floor of Vance Tower was no longer a cold, sterile fortress. 

Warm ambient sconces illuminated plush charcoal velvet sofas, vibrant modern oil paintings hung from the mahogany paneling, and the large glass executive desk near the floor-to-ceiling windows now featured two matching leather executive chairs side by side.

Nora Mercer-Vance stood by the floor-to-ceiling glass window, wearing a tailored cream silk trousers suit, sipping an espresso as she reviewed a printed draft of her upcoming investigative book on corporate syndicate corruption—a book that had already secured a six-figure publishing contract, national news features, and international translation rights.

On her left hand, the six-carat emerald-cut diamond ring caught the afternoon sun, sparkling with brilliant, electric fire alongside her simple platinum wedding band.

Behind her, a pair of strong, familiar arms wrapped possessively around her waist.

Killian buried his face in the crook of her neck, pressing a warm, lingering kiss to her skin, his long hands coming to rest gently over her stomach. He wore his crisp white dress shirt without a tie, the top buttons unfastened, his dark hair falling slightly over his forehead.

"You're working late, Mrs. Vance," Killian purred softly, his silver-blue eyes gleaming in the reflection of the dusk-lit glass.

"I have a deadline, Mr. Vance," Nora smiled, leaning back into his solid, muscular chest. "My publisher wants the final manuscript on my editor's desk by nine tomorrow morning."

"Your publisher can wait," Killian murmured, sliding his hand up her ribcage, pulling her body flush against his. "My chief of security reports that my wife hasn't given me a proper kiss in over three hours. That is a critical security breach."

Nora laughed softly, turning around in his arms, wrapping her hands around his linen collar, gazing up into his handsome face.

"Is that an executive threat, Lord Vance?"

Killian smiled—a real, breathtaking, handsome smile that transformed his ruthless, dangerous face into something divine.

"It's a promise, Little Bird," Killian whispered.

He reached into his jacket pocket, pulling out a small velvet-lined box. Inside lay a set of gold keys to a private four-story brownstone in Greenwich Village—a historic home he had bought in her name, featuring a light-filled two-story library, floor-to-ceiling bookshelves, and a private walled garden with white roses.

"You bought me a home," Nora whispered, looking up into his eyes, her amber eyes filling with warm tears of joy.

"You built *me* a home, Nora," Killian corrected softly, his silver eyes shining with endless, fierce devotion. "Before you walked into my penthouse with a stolen decrypter, I was just a ghost holding a gun in an empty tower. You gave me a soul."

Killian lifted her easily into his arms, carrying her toward their private suite as the sunset turned the sky over Manhattan into a magnificent sea of gold, crimson, and deep violet. 

As she rested her head against his shoulder, Nora looked out at the city lights beginning to awaken, sparkling like a million fallen stars. The world outside was vast, chaotic, and dangerous, but within these glass walls and in the circle of his arms, she was completely safe. She had entered his world looking for vengeance, and instead, she had found a love that was absolute, possessive, and beautiful in its complexity.

They had walked through blood, secrets, betrayal, and dark shadows to find each other. But in each other's arms, they had forged an unshakeable fortress of power, dangerous passion, and a hard-won, eternal forever. There would be no more cages, and no more running. They were bound by vicious ties, and neither of them would ever want to be free.
