# Chapter 8: The Ambush & Touch Her and Die

The Portland docks were shrouded in a thick, suffocating Atlantic fog that smelled of sea salt, cold rain, and heavy diesel fumes from cargo ships anchored out in the harbor channel. 

Three dark Suburban SUVs pulled up quietly behind Pier 14, their headlights extinguished, their high-powered engines idling silently against the ocean mist.

Inside the lead vehicle, Nora sat in the backseat, wearing a dark Kevlar vest under her leather jacket, her hand tightly gripping the Glock 19 Killian had given her. 

Beside her, Killian checked his weapon one last time. He wore a full black tactical suit, looking like the god of vengeance himself. His silver-blue eyes were cold, precise, and lethal.

"Stay in the vehicle, Nora," Killian repeated, his voice low and firm. "Marcus will guard the vehicle doors. Do not step onto the pier until the warehouse is cleared."

"Killian," Nora whispered, reaching out to catch his tactical sleeve. "Be careful out there. Don't take unnecessary risks."

Killian leaned over, cupping her nape, his lips crashing against hers in a fierce, possessive kiss that left her breathless and burning. "I'll be back in ten minutes, Little Bird. I have a wedding to plan."

Killian and eight Vance operators moved into the fog like shadows.

Minutes ticked by like hours. Inside the massive steel warehouse at the end of the pier, heavy gunfire erupted—the deafening roar of automatic shotguns and suppressed rifles echoing across the dark harbor waters. Flares of yellow light flickered against the fogged windows of the warehouse.

Nora held her breath, her knuckles white around the rubber grip of her gun, her heart thumping against her ribs like a drum.

Suddenly, the rear passenger door of the SUV was yanked open with violent force. 

A large, scarred hitman wearing a Moretti Syndicate jacket lunged inside, grabbing Nora by the wrist and dragging her out onto the wet asphalt!

"Got the bitch!" the hitman yelled into his shoulder radio. "Moretti, I've got Vance's girl!"

Nora didn't panic. Her survival instincts kicked in instantly. She brought her heavy leather boot down on the hitman's instep, driving her elbow hard into his broken nose. 

The hitman cursed loudly, releasing her wrist as blood gushed from his nostrils. 

Nora raised her Glock, firing a single round into his shoulder. The hitman collapsed back against a steel shipping container with a heavy groan.

Before she could turn back toward the vehicle, a second hitman stepped out from behind a container, raising a heavy steel crowbar to strike her.

A voice roared from the fog inside Pier 14—a sound of pure, apocalyptic fury.

"TOUCH HER AGAIN AND I WILL SKIN YOU ALIVE!"

It was Killian.

He emerged from the fog like a demon unleashed from hell. His obsidian eyes burned with pure, murderous fury. He didn't even use his firearm; he closed the distance in two strides, disarming the second hitman with brutal, terrifying efficiency, slamming him against the steel shipping container with bone-crushing force until the man fell unconscious to the wet asphalt.

Killian stood over the fallen hitmen, his chest heaving, his hands dripping with dark blood. 

He turned toward Nora, his protective rage fading into a look of sheer, frantic panic. He rushed over to her, his hands frantically checking her face, her shoulders, her waist for injuries.

"Did he hurt you?" Killian growled, his voice trembling with terrifying emotion. "Did he touch you?"

"I'm fine, Killian!" Nora insisted, grabbing his blood-stained hands, pressing them to her cheeks. "I'm okay! I shot him!"

Killian pulled her violently into his arms, burying his face in her neck, his body shaking with the force of his possessive love.

"If anyone ever lays a hand on you," Killian whispered fiercely against her skin, "I will burn their entire bloodline to ashes."

Marcus and the strike team emerged from the warehouse entrance, signaling that the interior was secured. 

Killian held Nora tight against his side, his hand resting over her hip as they walked together into the warehouse to face Victor Moretti.
