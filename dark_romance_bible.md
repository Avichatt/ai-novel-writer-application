# 🖤 Story Bible: *Vicious Ties*

## 1. Overview & Core Concept
- **Title**: *Vicious Ties*
- **Subgenre**: Contemporary Dark Romance / Psychological Suspense Romance
- **Target Audience**: New Adult / Adult Dark Romance Readers (BookTok & Dark Romance Enthusiasts)
- **Word Count Target**: 10,000+ words
- **Core Tropes**: Enemies to Lovers, Forced Proximity, Obsessive Love, Morally Gray Hero, Possessive Hero, Antihero, Forbidden Romance, Dangerous Attraction, Touch Her and Die, He Falls First & Hard, Slow Burn, Mutual Obsession.
- **Dark Elements**: Crime/Underworld syndicate, psychological power struggle, secrets, betrayal, revenge, trauma, power imbalance, possessiveness, high stakes.
- **Tone**: Dark, intense, atmospheric, suspenseful, sexy, psychological, emotional, dangerous, morally complex, addictive.
- **Ending**: Hard-won HEA with redemption, loyalty, and mutual surrender.

---

## 2. Main Characters

### Killian Vance (Hero)
- **Age**: 32
- **Role**: Head of the Vance Syndicate, Billionaire Financier & Underworld Sovereign.
- **Appearance**: 6'3", lean muscular build, sharp jawline with shadow stubble, silver-blue eyes like ice under glass, dark hair cut close at the sides. Tailored Tom Ford suits over obsidian tattoos covering his back and shoulders.
- **Personality**: Controlling, ruthless, hyper-intelligent, emotionally damaged, fiercely protective, deeply obsessive. He doesn't compromise; he conquers.
- **Psychological Arc**: Fallen first and fallen hard. He noticed Nora six months ago during her investigation, watching her from the shadows. When she steps into his trap, he pretends it's business, but he is already consumed by his obsession with her. He must learn that true possession is not breaking her, but earning her devotion.

### Nora Mercer (Heroine)
- **Age**: 26
- **Role**: Defiant Investigative Reporter & Art Archivist.
- **Appearance**: 5'7", honey-brown hair usually tied in a messy knot, expressive amber eyes, sharp cheekbones, understated elegance. 
- **Personality**: Fiercely independent, highly resilient, sarcastic, brave, emotionally complex. She refuses to cower before powerful men.
- **Psychological Arc**: Investigating her father's suspicious "accidental" death leads her directly into Killian's lion den. Under forced proximity, she fights her forbidden attraction to the man who holds her fate in his hands, only to discover that her hatred hides a dark, intoxicating obsession of her own.

### Supporting Characters
1. **Marcus Kane**: Killian's ruthless right-hand man and security director. Cold, hyper-efficient, loyal to Killian above all.
2. **Victor Moretti**: Boss of the rival Moretti Crime Family. Cruel, opportunistic, seeking to destroy Vance's empire.

---

## 3. Setting & Locations
1. **The Manhattan Penthouse**: Killian's glass-walled fortress overlooking the NYC skyline. Cold steel, leather, state-of-the-art security, and floor-to-ceiling windows.
2. **Blackwood Isle Manor**: A remote, cliffside estate on the coast of Maine. Surrounded by pine forests, crashing ocean waves, and high stone walls. A luxurious cage where the forced proximity unfolds.

---

## 4. Narrative & Relationship Trajectory

- **Act I (Chapters 1–3)**: The Break-In, The Trap & Terms of Surrender. High psychological tension, power imbalance, forced removal to Blackwood Manor.
- **Act II (Chapters 4–7)**: Forced Proximity, Psychological Games & Escalating Obsession. The slow burn explodes into raw physical intimacy and shared trauma.
- **Act III (Chapters 8–10)**: Syndicate War, "Touch Her and Die" Retaliation & Hard-Won HEA. Killian risks everything to protect Nora; Nora chooses him willingly.
